We're buying the minerals under a 480-acre tract, Sec 88, Blk 4, T&P Ry Co Survey,
A-1204. Offer is $310,000. Seller is a family LLC.

Our attorney ran a title opinion three weeks ago and came back with nine requirements.
We've already chased four of them — got two corrective affidavits signed and recorded, got
a release on a 2011 deed of trust, and confirmed the 2003 acreage variance was a resurvey
artifact. The other five are still open and the seller's patience is thinning.

The five open ones:

1. **Federal tax lien**, filed 2009 against Marvin Ostroff, who held the minerals
   1998–2013. Notice of Federal Tax Lien, Doc. 2009-04471, $118,000. No release of record.
   Our attorney flagged this one hardest — said it "travels with the land."

2. **Unreleased oil and gas lease**, recorded 1981 to Sandridge Petroleum, primary term
   five years, no Pugh clause. Covers our tract among 2,400 other acres. No release, no
   production on our tract that anyone can find.

3. **Reservation language**, Warranty Deed 1961, Vol 212 Pg 88, Ostroff Sr. to Ostroff
   Jr.: *"reserving unto Grantor an undivided one-half of the one-eighth royalty."*
   Attorney's note says "construction uncertain, may be 1/16 or 1/2 depending."

4. **Entity authority gap** — the 2013 deed into the current family LLC was signed by a
   managing member, but no operating agreement or resolution is of record showing she had
   authority. She's alive, cooperative, and lives in Amarillo.

5. **Name variance** — "Marvin J. Ostroff" on the 1998 deed in, "M. Jay Ostroff" on the
   2013 deed out. Same person by all appearances.

The seller wants to close in 45 days. Our landman says we can probably knock out three of
these in a week. I need to know whether to keep spending or restructure the offer.
